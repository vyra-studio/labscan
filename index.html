What is LabScan?

The lab report scanner that speaks your language

LabScan: The Report Scanner is a free Android app by VYRA Studio. Take a photo of any lab report — blood test, sugar test, thyroid panel and more — or upload its PDF, and the built-in assistant Sehat AI explains every value in simple words: what the test means, whether your result is normal, and what to do next. No medical degree needed.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is LabScan?

LabScan: The Report Scanner is a free Android app by VYRA Studio. You take a photo of your lab report or upload its PDF, and its built-in assistant, Sehat AI, explains every test value in simple, easy words — what the test is, whether your value is normal, and what it means for your health.

How does LabScan explain my lab report?

Open LabScan, tap Sehat AI, and scan or upload your report. Sehat AI reads the values, compares them with normal reference ranges, and explains each result in plain language. Values that need attention are flagged, and critical results come with clear advice to see a doctor.

Which languages does LabScan support?

LabScan explains your reports in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu, so you can read your results in the language you understand best.

Is LabScan free?

Yes. LabScan is free to download and use, including report explanations, Health Score, manual test checks and medicine reminders.

Is my health data private in LabScan?

Yes. LabScan has no server of its own — your reports and health data are stored only on your phone. Report explanations are generated only when you ask Sehat AI to explain a report. See the LabScan Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I install LabScan on Android?

Tap the Download button on this page to get the LabScan APK and open the downloaded file. If Android shows a security popup, tap Settings on the popup, turn on "Allow from this source", go back and tap Install. The whole process takes less than a minute — see the step-by-step box above. LabScan is also coming soon to Google Play.

Does LabScan replace a doctor?

No. LabScan helps you understand your reports in simple words, but it does not diagnose or treat any condition. Always consult a qualified doctor about your results — especially when LabScan flags a value as critical.
